Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

1. About this app

Memo Pin Scheduler ("the App") is a personal software tool built and run by an individual operator located in Japan ("the Operator"). The App is used only by the Operator, only with the Operator's own Pinterest business accounts, through the official Pinterest API. The App is not offered to the public and has no other users.

2. What the App does

The App lets the Operator (a) create and schedule Pins — using images, titles, descriptions and destination links that the Operator has prepared in advance — on the Operator's own Pinterest boards, and (b) read analytics (such as impressions, saves and outbound clicks) for the Operator's own accounts.

3. Information the App handles

  • Pinterest OAuth tokens (access token and refresh token) issued when the Operator authorizes the App on their own Pinterest accounts.
  • Pin content prepared by the Operator: images, titles, descriptions and destination links submitted to the Pinterest API for publication on the Operator's own boards.
  • Analytics data of the Operator's own accounts returned by the Pinterest API.

The App does not collect, receive or process personal data of any third party. No Pinterest user other than the Operator connects to the App.

4. How the information is used

The tokens are used only to call the Pinterest API on the Operator's own behalf for the purposes described in section 2. Pin content is transmitted to Pinterest for publication. Analytics data is used only by the Operator to review the performance of their own accounts.

5. Storage and retention

OAuth tokens are stored on the Operator's own systems with access restricted to the Operator. Tokens are kept only while the App is in use. When the App is discontinued, or when the Operator revokes the App's access in their Pinterest settings, the stored tokens are deleted and can no longer be used.

6. Sharing

The Operator does not sell, rent or share any information handled by the App with third parties. Information is sent only to Pinterest, as required for the API calls described above.

7. Revoking access

The Operator can revoke the App's access at any time from the Pinterest account settings (Apps / authorized apps). Revocation immediately stops the App from accessing the account.
